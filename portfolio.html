deep learning · ML for BI 2 · 2026

Grocery product classification with CNNs

Classifying photos of grocery products into 25 categories (Freiburg Groceries). I compared a convnet built from scratch, developed step by step from a baseline through augmentation to residual blocks, with a fine-tuned EfficientNetV2B0.

82.2 %test accuracy, fine-tuned EfficientNetV2B0 (vs 64.6 % from scratch)
  • Regularisation alone doubled validation accuracy over the overfitting baseline
  • Grad-CAM shows the from-scratch model relies on colour and shape, not texture

Python / TensorFlow / Keras / transfer learning / Grad-CAM

02

machine learning · Data Science Project · 2026

Predicting Danish building energy labels

A group project that predicts a building's energy label (A–G) from 2,205 official energy-label reports for Aarhus. We scraped the reports and parsed them from XML. Two models were compared: one using the full report, and one using structural features only, which could be applied to buildings without a report.

68 %test accuracy with gradient boosting, weighted F1 0.69 across 7 imbalanced classes
  • End-to-end pipeline: web scraping, XML parsing, cleaning and feature engineering
  • U-values of walls, windows and roof, and building age, are the strongest structural predictors

Python / scikit-learn / web scraping / gradient boosting

03

bachelor's thesis · VŠE Prague · 2025

Twitter sentiment and investment decisions

Does sentiment on Twitter move with the returns and volatility of Apple, Microsoft, Tesla, Amazon, Alphabet and Bitcoin? Tweets are scored with VADER, volatility is modelled with GARCH and EGARCH, and the two are linked through lagged correlation, cross-correlation and wavelet coherence.

13.9 Mtweets analysed: 13.8 M on Bitcoin, 52 k on the five stocks
  • Significant sentiment–price links for Tesla, Bitcoin and Apple, but not for Microsoft, Amazon or Alphabet
  • On a daily frequency, sentiment mostly follows past price moves rather than predicting them

Python / NLP / VADER / GARCH / wavelet analysis
